About the Academy

Sport development with a campus heartbeat

The SRC Sports Academy is a Pentecost University SRC initiative focused on structured training, discipline, community engagement, and competitive growth.

Mission

Develop athletes through consistent coaching, safe participation, teamwork, and confidence-building programs.

Vision

Become a trusted university sports academy that supports talent from beginner level to competition readiness.

Values

Excellence, integrity, community, discipline, and respect shape every training session and event.

Funding and support

The academy is backed by SRC leadership, Pentecost University support, community sports bodies, regional partners, and private sponsorship opportunities.

  • University Support
  • Community Partners
