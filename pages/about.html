About SRC Sports Academy

Excellence, Community, and Competitive Development

Who We Are

The SRC Sports Academy is a groundbreaking initiative launched by the Student Representative Council of Pentecost University. Our mission is to develop sporting excellence while promoting sports development within the university community and beyond.

With secured funding and strong institutional support, we're committed to building a sustainable sports ecosystem that nurtures talent, promotes discipline, and fosters community engagement.

Our Core Values

Mission

Develop world-class sports programs that unlock human potential through discipline, teamwork, and competitive excellence.

Vision

Become a leading regional sports institution recognized for developing champions and fostering sports excellence.
